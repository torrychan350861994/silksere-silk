Silkser – Premium Mulberry Silk Clothing

Silkser is a fashion brand specializing in 100% mulberry silk clothing for modern women. Our collections include silk dresses, blouses, and sleepwear designed for elegance, comfort, and skin-friendly wear.

Why Choose Silk?

Mulberry silk is known for its softness, breathability, and natural ability to regulate temperature. Compared to synthetic fabrics, silk is more comfortable and better for sensitive skin.

Silk vs Satin

Silk is a natural fiber, while satin is a weaving technique often made from synthetic materials. Silk provides better breathability and long-term comfort.
